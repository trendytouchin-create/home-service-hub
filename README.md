# Home Service Hub

System & Role: Act as an expert frontend developer and mobile UI/UX designer. Build a strictly mobile-first, single-page website for a local home services business offering AC Repair, Plumbing, and Electrical services. The design must feel native and app-like on mobile devices, prioritizing thumb-friendly navigation, touch-optimized targets, and lightning-fast load times.

Design Reference:

Inspiration Link: [https://dribbble.com/shots/27520639-Home-Service-Landing-Page-Design?utm_source=Clipboard_Shot&utm_campaign=helalhossain08&utm_content=Home%20Service%20Landing%20Page%20Design&utm_medium=Social_Share&utm_source=Clipboard_Shot&utm_campaign=helalhossain08&utm_content=Home%20Service%20Landing%20Page%20Design&utm_medium=Social_Share]

Instruction: Emulate the overall aesthetic and layout of this link, but prioritize its mobile view. Adapt the design principles to ensure a flawless experience on small screens.

Website Sections & Requirements (Mobile-Optimized):

Header & Navigation Bar: Use a sticky top header with a clean Hamburger Menu for navigation. Include a prominent, always-visible "Tap to Call" icon/button that stays fixed at the top or bottom of the mobile screen.

Hero Section: Center-aligned, highly legible text for small screens (e.g., "Reliable Home Services at Your Doorstep"). Use a compressed, fast-loading background image and a massive, thumb-friendly "Book Now" primary CTA button.

Services Section: Instead of a complex grid, use a vertical stack or a horizontal touch-swipeable carousel for the three main services (AC Repair, Plumbing, Electrical). Include large icons and expandable "Read More" text to save screen space.

Why Choose Us: A compact 2x2 grid or swipeable list featuring trust signals with clear icons (24/7 Availability, Certified Experts, Upfront Pricing).

Service Areas & Location: A clean, collapsible list of areas served and a tap-to-open map integration (Google Maps link) rather than a heavy embedded map that slows down mobile scrolling.

Gallery & Proof of Work: A touch-friendly, swipeable image slider showing "Before & After" photos. Avoid small thumbnails that are hard to tap.

Contact & Enquiry Form: Optimize for mobile typing. Use large, thumb-friendly input fields. Set proper HTML input types (e.g., type="tel" so the mobile number pad pops up automatically). Keep the form short and vertically aligned.

FAQ Section: A smooth, touch-friendly accordion layout. Only one question should expand at a time to prevent endless scrolling on mobile.

Footer: Stacked minimalist layout containing quick links, social icons with large tap targets, and essential legal links.

Website Quality & Technical Essentials:

Thumb Zone Optimization: Ensure all interactive elements (buttons, links, form fields) are at least 44x44 pixels and placed within easy reach of the thumb.

Performance: Optimize for slow 3G/4G networks. Implement lazy loading for images and use lightweight CSS/JS.

Animations: Keep scroll animations subtle and hardware-accelerated so they don't cause lag on older smartphones. Avoid hover effects, replacing them with active/tap states.
